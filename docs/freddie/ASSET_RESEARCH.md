## SEARCH TIPS FOR FURTHER HUNTING

- **Sketchfab.com:** Search "low poly cat" / "low poly city" and filter by **Downloadable** + **Creative Commons** — many free models available
- **Fab.com (Unity section):** Search "low poly urban props"
- **Unity Asset Store:** The old assetstore.unity.com has been merged into Fab.com
- **itch.io:** Search terms that worked well: "low poly city props", "low poly cat", "low poly rat", "low poly industrial"
